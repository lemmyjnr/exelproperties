Our Story

Every Client Wins Because
Every Client Matters

At ExelProperties Limited, we believe buying property should be an exciting milestone rather than a stressful experience. For over two decades, we have helped individuals, families, and investors make confident real estate decisions by connecting them with carefully selected opportunities from trusted developers.

Rather than simply selling properties, we take time to understand every client's goals, budget, and long-term vision. We provide honest advice, transparent guidance, and end-to-end support throughout the buying journey.

Every client wins because every client matters.

Mission Statement

The Founder

Meet ESV Iluyinka Adeyemi

Founder & Principal Advisor, ExelProperties Limited

Known across Lagos as "Lagos' Most Caring Realtor," ESV Iluyinka Adeyemi has spent over 20 years guiding families and investors through some of the most important financial decisions of their lives — with patience, integrity and an unwavering promise that every client wins.

What began as a personal mission to make property ownership honest and accessible has grown into ExelProperties Limited — a trusted advisory bridging everyday Nigerians and diaspora investors with credible developers across Lagos and beyond.
